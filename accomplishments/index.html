THE CLUB CHARTER

Very few rules.
Very many honors.

This club exists to leave you feeling a little more pleased with yourself.

01

Small is the whole point.

Ordinary effort deserves recognition. You don’t need to earn your way into feeling good.

02

Repeat honors encouraged.

You may celebrate the same thing more than once. Laundry keeps happening.

03

No guilt. Ever.

No streaks, rankings, deadlines, or overdue anything. The club will be here when you return.

04

Your glory, your business.

Awards stay in this browser unless you choose to share an image. Your name is always optional.
