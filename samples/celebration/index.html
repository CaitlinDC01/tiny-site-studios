Quick answers

Graduation weekend FAQ.

Do I need a ceremony ticket?

Yes. Each guest needs a digital ticket. Save it to your phone before arriving because reception can be spotty inside.

Where should I park?

Use the west garage on Main Street. Accessible spaces are on level one near the elevator.

Can I bring flowers or a gift?

Absolutely. A card basket and flower table will be available at dinner so you do not have to carry them through the ceremony.

What if I am only coming to dessert?

Join us at 8:15 PM. Use the side entrance marked Garden Room; no dinner RSVP is needed for the open-house portion.

How do I share my photos?

Use the shared-album button in the guest guide. Please add your favorites before Monday evening.
