Drop a handful at once.
Drag images or documents from your computer. Each file becomes its own card, up to 15 MB each and 30 items per drop.

Save from your browser.
Drag a link, linked image, or address-bar URL onto the page. Tab dragging depends on your browser; copying the tab’s URL always works.

Paste a public Pin’s link or drag its linked image. Short pin.it links work on saved boards. Secret pins may need an uploaded screenshot. This saves individual pins; it doesn’t connect or sync your Pinterest account.
