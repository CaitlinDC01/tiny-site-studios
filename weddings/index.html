Before you ask

Helpful answers.

If you still aren’t sure what you need, that’s completely fine. Helping you sort it out is part of the service.

Why would I pay when wedding websites are free?+

Because you are paying to remove the project from your list. You send what you have; we organize it, design it, configure the tools, test it, and give you a finished site.

What do I need to have ready?+

Whatever you already have. Photos, venue links, invitation designs, registry links, schedules, screenshots, Pinterest boards, and unfinished notes are all welcome. It does not need to be organized.

Can my site be bilingual?+

Yes. A bilingual build is a $19 add-on. We use AI-assisted translation into one additional language, and you proofread and approve the final wording before launch.

How long does my site stay online?+

Essentials and Signature include six months of hosting. Wedding Hub and Full Experience include one year. Continued hosting is available at the current domain and hosting cost, usually $15–$25 per year.

Do I need a custom domain?+

No. Every site receives a shareable link. A standard custom domain is a $19 add-on for Essentials and Signature and is included with Wedding Hub and Full Experience.

What if our details change?+

Your package includes revision rounds before launch. Full Experience also includes limited launch support. Additional updates can be quoted when needed.
