01 · When you’re invited

First, a very happy yes.

Being beside us means more than matching outfits. We’re asking for your friendship, your presence, and honest conversations about what works for you.

  • Rehearsal: Sunday, October 3, at 4:30 PM.

  • Extra celebrations are optional. Please never stretch your budget to attend.

02 · Once you’ve accepted

Get the big things settled.

Dates to hold

By March 1
Confirm participation, preferred name, and the best way to reach you.
By May 1
Share outfit sizing privately with your group lead.
By July 1
Book your travel and lodging once your plans are confirmed.
September 1
RSVP and share dietary, mobility, or other access needs.

Travel & staying nearby

Fly into Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport. Plan to arrive before Sunday’s rehearsal; Saturday’s welcome toast is optional.

Our sample bases are Audubon Cottages and Maison de la Luz. No room block or booking code is active in this demo.

Arrange your airport ride and hotel booking. Wedding-day group transport is provided from the getting-ready locations.

What we’re covering

  • Bouquets, boutonnières, and pocket squares
  • Getting-ready breakfast/lunch as listed below
  • Hair and makeup for Bea’s party
  • Rehearsal dinner and wedding-day group transport
  • Flower basket and practice ring pillow

What to plan for

  • Your outfit or rental, alterations, shoes, and accessories
  • Travel, hotel, and meals outside hosted events
  • Optional pre-wedding celebrations

Confirm costs with Bea or Milo before purchasing. If the budget is uncomfortable, tell us privately—we’ll work it out together. No additional gift is expected.

Who handles what?

Camille coordinates Bea’s party; Theo coordinates Milo’s party. They collect questions and help keep everyone on the same page. June is the day-of coordinator. Use the contact list for sample contact options.

Outfit deadlines

May 1: confirm sizing and style with Camille or Theo.
June 1: order dress or reserve tux.

September 20: final fitting with your actual shoes.

Check availability and final costs with your group lead before ordering. No shop orders are placed through this demo.

Little-star outfits

Ella: a comfortable ivory dress and secure flat shoes. Oliver: a light shirt, navy trousers, and comfortable closed-toe shoes. Their guardians confirm fit by September 20 and bring a change of clothes.

Optional celebrations

Shower and bachelor/bachelorette plans are shared by the hosts once dates and costs are agreed. Participation is optional; ask before booking anything nonrefundable.

By September 1

Confirm your invited household, meal needs, allergies, access needs, and travel arrival. Camille and Theo can help with changes. Send sensitive details privately rather than in a group chat.

Ceremony & the celebration

Everyone has a place.

Camille & Theo

Camille helps with Bea’s bouquet during vows. Theo holds the real rings; Oliver carries a practice pillow. Both check in with June before speeches. Everyone else follows June’s cues and helps gather for photos.

Ella & Oliver’s grown-ups

Ella’s guardian Marianne Laurent and Oliver’s guardian Henry Bennett remain responsible throughout. Bring them at 3:30 PM, stay for photos, and sit near the aisle. If either child opts out, their guardian walks with them or they sit out—no pressure.

After walking, both children sit with their guardians. Parents handle meals, bathroom trips, and departure; the wedding party is not a childcare service.

After “I do”

Follow June’s second-line and reception-transfer directions. Cocktails at Maison de la Luz from 5:30 PM; grand entrance at 6:30, dinner at 6:45, toasts at 7:30, dancing from 8.

Return shuttles use the Carondelet Street entrance every 20 minutes from 10 PM through the 11 PM send-off. Children may leave earlier with their guardians. Keep your belongings together and check the green room before leaving.
