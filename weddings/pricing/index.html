The fine print, in plain language

Hosting, domains, and updates.

Hosting period

Essentials and Signature include six months. Wedding Hub and Full Experience include one year.

After hosting ends

Continue at the current GoDaddy cost—usually $15–$25 per year—or request a transfer of your domain and site files.

Domain registration

A custom domain is registered for one year. Standard available domains are included where stated; premium domains cost extra.

Revision rounds

Each round is one consolidated list of requested changes. This keeps revisions clear, fast, and affordable.

Translated content

Tiny Site Studios uses ChatGPT for the first translation. You are responsible for proofreading and final approval.

Changing details

Pre-launch changes are covered by your revision rounds. Additional or post-launch changes can be quoted as needed.
