The person behind the words

Always a bridesmaid.
Now an officiant.

You tell me your story.
I’ll help you find the words.

I’ve always loved love. The strange first dates. The inside jokes. The ordinary Tuesday nights. The little moments when someone becomes your person.

I’ve loved words for just as long. I was the kid volunteering to read aloud in class, and I’ve spent years writing meaningful letters, speeches, and messages for the people I care about.

A few years ago, I wrote and gave a speech at my best friend’s wedding. Standing there, sharing her story on one of the biggest days of her life, something clicked: I could do this.

So now I do. I’m Caitlin, and I write and officiate ceremonies that feel honest, meaningful, and completely yours.

A few good questions

Before we
say “I do.”

Do we have to be religious?

No. I welcome religious, spiritual, secular, and interfaith ceremonies. Tell me what feels right and what you’d like left out.

Will you officiate LGBTQ+ weddings?

Joyfully. Your love belongs here, and your ceremony will use the names, pronouns, and language that feel right to you.

Where do you officiate?

I’m based in the Houston area. Share your venue and city when you inquire so I can confirm availability and any travel costs.

Can we write our own vows?

Absolutely. Personal vows, repeat-after-me vows, or a mix of both are welcome. Your Story includes a vow-writing guide and feedback; The Full Love Story includes coaching for both partners.

What if we don’t know what to say?

That’s why I’m here. Notes, stories, and half-finished thoughts are a great starting point. You don’t need polished answers to have a meaningful ceremony.

Can we keep some stories private?

Yes. We’ll discuss what can be shared and what is off limits. Private partner questionnaires include a chance to flag answers that should stay between us.

Is a wedding website required?

No. Ceremony services stand on their own. If you’d also like help with your wedding website, Guestbook Live, or planning tools, those are separate options.
