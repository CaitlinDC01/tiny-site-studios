Can I afford this wedding?

Tell us the budget, guest count, market, and type of wedding you’re picturing. We’ll show whether the plan looks comfortable, close, or stretched—and which decisions can move the numbers the most.

This is a reality check, not a verdict.

The goal is not to tell you what your wedding “should” cost. It is to show which assumptions are driving the budget so you can change the plan before contracts make those choices expensive.
