Where should the money actually go?

Start with the budget you already have—or let us give you a realistic planning range. Then we’ll divide it around your guest count and the things you care about most.

This is not another generic percentage chart.

Wedding costs vary wildly by guest count, location, venue model, and priorities. This tool gives you a starting framework you can replace with real quotes as you book.
