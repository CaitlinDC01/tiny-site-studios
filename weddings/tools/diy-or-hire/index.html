DIY it... or hire it?

DIY can save money. It can also turn into 40 hours of work, a living room full of supplies, or a problem that has to be solved on your wedding day. Let’s figure out which projects actually make sense for you.

“Can I do it?” is only one question.

We’ll also look at how long it takes, how many guests you have, how close the wedding is, whether you have help, and how expensive a mistake would be.
