The practical details

Before your guests scan.

Do guests need an app or account?

No. They scan your QR code and contribute from their phone’s browser without creating an account.

Can we control what appears publicly?

Yes. You can require host approval before a contribution appears on the public wall or live slideshow.

Can remote guests or attendees contribute?

Yes. Share the same event link with anyone you want to invite to contribute, whether they are in the room or not.

How does the live slideshow work?

Open the presentation view on a laptop connected to a TV or projector. Approved memories rotate automatically, and newly approved posts refresh during the event.

What do we receive afterward?

Your host dashboard remains available for 12 months. You can download individual originals or the complete collection, including a written transcript, in one archive.

Can it match our event branding?

Guestbook Live includes branded frame options. More extensive coordination with your event identity or a custom event site can be quoted separately.
