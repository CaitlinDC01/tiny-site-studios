Helpful answers

Before you scan.

The practical details hosts and guests usually want to know.

Do guests need an app or account?

No. Guests scan your QR code and contribute from their phone’s web browser without creating an account.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Guestbook Live is designed for current mobile browsers on both iPhone and Android. Guests can take or choose photos, record or choose a short video, record a voice message, or write a note.

Can people contribute if they cannot attend?

Yes. Send them the same private event link. Contributions remain open through 90 days after the event unless the host closes the guestbook sooner.

Can we control what appears on the live guestbook?

Yes. Host approval can be required before a contribution appears publicly. Your dashboard also lets you edit, hide, or remove posts.

What happens to our files afterward?

The host dashboard remains available for 12 months. Hosts can download individual originals or save the complete collection, including a written transcript, as one ZIP archive.

How does the live slideshow work?

Open the presentation view on a laptop connected to a TV or projector. Approved memories rotate automatically, new approved posts refresh during the event, and an optional QR stays visible for guests.

What is in the participation kit?

Your dashboard creates event-specific printable signs and table cards plus ready-to-use DJ or MC announcement, text-reminder, and post-event reminder copy.

Does Guestbook Live replace a photographer?

No. It gathers the candid angles, voices, and messages around the professional record of your event.
